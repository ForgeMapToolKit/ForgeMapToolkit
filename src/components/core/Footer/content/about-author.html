About the Author

I go by Seraphim-noob in the FAF community. My history with Supreme Commander actually goes back further than that name suggests. My father bought the original game when I was a kid and I played it back then, long before I knew anything about Forged Alliance Forever or the competitive scene around it.

My first real game in FAF was on January 22, 2013, but that connection didn't stick right away. It took about ten years before I came back to the game with any real intent. I started playing again on December 21, 2022, this time with the goal of actually improving instead of just playing casually.

From there things moved quickly. I joined the trainer team on May 25, 2024, and reached 2000 rating on July 8, 2024. More recently, on March 7, 2026, I joined the FAF Association.

From Player to Developer

My path into development started small and almost by accident. I wrote a handful of Python scripts to speed up wreckage and emitter placement on maps I was working on, nothing meant to become a project. Around the same time, the FAF community was going through a shortage of active developers, and I ended up filling some of that gap without really planning to. Those early scripts eventually grew into ForgeMapToolkit.
